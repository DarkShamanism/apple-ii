FOR PASSING TRAVELERS

The Guestbook.

A book kept on this site. Leave a note, even when the workshop is quiet: where you are reading from, what machine is on your desk, what you are building.

Sign with a name and a few words. Nothing else is asked for.

Notes keep to the letters an Apple II can show; anything else becomes a question mark. No web addresses. One note every two minutes, five a day. Your name is remembered in this browser only. No account, no password, no cookies.

The book

Opening the book.

From your Apple II

Through Farglade's PC uplink, with apple.crowbarsworkshop.com allowed in its NetHosts list: RUN BROWSER and open apple.crowbarsworkshop.com/lite/guestbook.php. Give a name with the page's form, then write your note with the next one.

The book is public: what is written in it can be read by anyone who opens it, and the newest 500 notes are kept. Be kind. No personal details.
