From your Apple II

Two ways, both through Farglade's PC uplink with apple.crowbarsworkshop.com allowed in its NetHosts list.

In the browser. Open apple.crowbarsworkshop.com/lite/chat.php, take a name with the page's form, then say a line with it.

In the wire program (Farglade OS 0.9.0 or later). The talk scrolls by itself there:

Workshop etiquette

Be welcoming. Use short, plain messages: the room keeps to the letters an Apple II can show. Ask before posting long logs. No harassment, flooding, or personal information. This is a public room and what is said in it can be read by anyone who opens it.
